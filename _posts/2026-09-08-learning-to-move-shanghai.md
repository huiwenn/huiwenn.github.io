[Our work](({{ '/learning-to-move-learning-to-play-learning-to-animate' | relative_url }})) is a part of the art exhibition at the [Inclusion Conference on the Bund](https://www.inclusionconf.com/) in Shanghai this year. [Mingyong](https://www.mingyongcheng.com/), [Han](https://zhanghanunwalled.com/), [Lindsey](https://www.yuemenglindseygu.com/) and I presented together as the artist group `0x0a`. Huge thanks to the curation team [;C](https://semicolonc.art/event/inclusion2026) for their support.
